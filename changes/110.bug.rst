Fix client/engine contract bugs in the offline DSL and response readers:
``mkPool`` now builds a usable pool object (single-type pools are tagged
``LPool``/``MPool``/…, mixed pools become ``UPool`` with ``AssetUnion``
assets) and already-tagged pools are passed through by ``runPool`` and
accepted by ``Generic``/``SPV``; single mixed-asset pools are detected and
tagged ``UPool`` instead of being mis-tagged from their first asset
(``Mortgage`` + ``Loan`` previously decoded as an ``MPool`` engine-side).
Pool collection statistics now emit the ``PoolCollectionStats`` tag the
engine actually defines (``PoolCurCollectionStats`` never existed engine-side
and made any ``curPoolCollectionStats``/``资产池当期至`` formula fail
server-side); the tuple variant of the ``and`` call-alias no longer collides
with its ``all`` twin, and the ``OffsetDatePattern`` alias typo is fixed.
A bond with ``rateType: None`` now serializes as ``Fix IRate DayCount``
(previously a day-count enum object was embedded in the rate slot). Chinese invoice
aliases accept both ``应收账款`` and ``应收帐款`` in single and mixed pools,
``ProjectedCashflow`` is supported by ``mkAssetUnion``, and the ``封包日``
cutoff key no longer leaks into pool requests. The resec pool-flow reader
parses ``<deal>:<bond>:<settle date>:<pct>`` keys correctly (previously every
call raised ``IndexError``) and survives ``:`` in deal names. ``mkRevolvingPool``
and ``mkPoolType`` raise ``AbsboxParseError`` on unknown input instead of
returning ``None``, ``mkDeal`` fails fast when the pool is missing, and
cashflow-only pools with no assets report a clear ``AbsboxParseError`` instead
of a ``TypeError``.
