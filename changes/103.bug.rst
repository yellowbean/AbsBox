Chinese invoice assets normalize consistently: 应收账款 (账) is the canonical
tag, and the 帐 / 賬 variants are accepted everywhere the Chinese tag appears
(``mkAsset``, ``mkAssetUnion``, pool-type inference aliases) and all spellings
map to the same engine ``Invoice`` asset (``RE`` in a union pool) — previously
``应收賬款`` failed outright and the two spellings drifted across call sites.
